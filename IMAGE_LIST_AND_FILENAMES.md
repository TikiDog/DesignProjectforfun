## 💡 **How to Take Screenshots**

### **On Mac:**
1. Press **Shift + Command + 4**
2. Press **Spacebar** (cursor turns into a camera)
3. Click the PDF window to capture the whole page
4. OR drag to select a specific area
5. Screenshot saves to Desktop

### **On Windows:**
1. Press **Windows + Shift + S**
2. Drag to select the area you want
3. Screenshot copies to clipboard
4. Open **Paint** and paste (Ctrl+V)
5. Save as JPEG with the filename from the list above

### **Best Practices:**
- **Quality:** Use high resolution (at least 1200px wide)
- **Format:** Save as JPEG (not PNG, to keep file size reasonable)
- **Naming:** Use exact filenames from the tables above (lowercase, hyphens, .jpg extension)
- **Content:** Screenshot the most visually appealing page of each project
- **Cropping:** Make sure images are clean with no extra borders or desktop elements

## 📤 **How to Upload to GitHub**

1. Go to your repository: https://github.com/TikiDog/DesignProjectforfun
2. Switch to branch: `claude/pdf-to-html-portfolio-Stm1i`
3. Navigate to the correct folder (e.g., `images/event-design/`)
4. Click **"Add file"** → **"Upload files"**
5. Drag your images or click to browse
6. Scroll down and click **"Commit changes"**

**OR upload in bulk:**
1. Go to the main `images/` folder
2. Upload all at once
3. They'll automatically go into the correct subfolders based on filename
