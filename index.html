About Me

I'm a Lead App Designer and Senior Production Graphic Artist with 20+ years of experience transforming technical content into intuitive, on-brand digital products that users love to engage with.

Note: The portfolio samples shown here represent my earlier work. For the past 7 years, I've been contracting for the U.S. Military, creating interactive training applications under security restrictions that prevent me from publicly displaying that work. Rest assured, my skills have only grown stronger through these challenging projects.

At StartGuides, I transform simple wireframes into interactive mobile training apps for the U.S. Military using Adobe InDesign's interactive capabilities. My mission: create "wow" user interface experiences that make complex military training accessible, engaging, and user-focused.

What I Bring to Every Project:

  • Deep InDesign expertise with a specialty in interactive features, epub production, branding, and long-form layouts
  • Strong UI design sensibility that prioritizes user experience
  • Brand stewardship—Create and maintain guidelines that ensure consistency across all touchpoints
  • Team leadership—Mentoring designers, conducting training, and keeping multiple projects on track and under budget
  • Process innovation—R&D enthusiast who constantly finds smarter workflows to save time and money. Proof: currently mastering Figma and Claude Code

Over 15 years as Senior Production Graphic Artist at Envision Technology Marketing Group, where I upheld brand standards for Fortune 500 clients, including Cisco, Adobe, Intel, Oracle, McAfee, and NVIDIA. From interactive PDFs to HTML newsletters to social media assets.

I lead by example and positivity, whether I'm managing workflows, collaborating with content developers, and mentoring new team members.
