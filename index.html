About Me

I'm a Lead App Designer and Senior Production Graphic Artist with over 20 years of experience turning technical content into intuitive, on-brand digital products that users actually want to engage with.

I design collaborative and innovative mobile training apps for the U.S. Military at StartGuides. Our apps begin with a simple wireframe. My mission is to transform a basic wireframe into an interactive app using Adobe InDesign's interactive capabilities. My focus is on creating a "wow" user interface experience that makes complex information accessible, engaging, and user-focused. Always consider the user!

What I Bring to Every Project:

  • Deep InDesign expertise with a specialty in interactive features, epub production, branding, and long-form layouts
  • Strong UI design sensibility that prioritizes user experience
  • Brand stewardship—Create and maintain guidelines that ensure consistency across all touchpoints
  • Team leadership—Mentoring designers, conducting training, and keeping multiple projects on track and under budget
  • Process innovation—I'm passionate about research and development, finding better ways to accomplish tasks and save time and money. I do believe old dogs can and will learn new tricks.

Over 15 years as Senior Production Graphic Artist at Envision Technology Marketing Group, where I upheld brand standards for Fortune 500 clients, including Cisco, Adobe, Intel, Oracle, McAfee, and NVIDIA. From interactive PDFs to HTML newsletters to social media assets.

I lead by example and positivity, whether I'm managing workflows, collaborating with content developers, or mentoring new team members. Currently, teaching myself Figma and dabbling in Claude Code.
