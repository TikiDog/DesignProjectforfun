About Me

20+ years transforming technical content into intuitive, on-brand digital products for Fortune 500 clients and the U.S. Military.

Why you see older samples: For the past 7 years, I've been creating classified interactive training applications for the U.S. Military. My portfolio stopped in 2019, but my expertise didn't—it just went behind a security clearance.

These samples show where I started. They don't show the complex, long-form apps I build today using InDesign's advanced interactive features, MSOs, and engaging UI experiences.

What I Do Best

  • InDesign Expert: Interactive PDFs, ePubs, branding, and long-form layouts
  • UI/UX Design: User-focused interfaces for mobile training apps
  • Brand Stewardship: Consistent guidelines across all touchpoints
  • Team Leadership: Mentoring designers and managing multiple projects
  • Process Innovation: Finding smarter workflows (currently mastering Figma & Claude Code)
